# Dale "The House" Kroft

The dealer at The House Always Wins. Everything he says in the game, from a
one-line roast to a five-minute story, fits this life. Write new lines against
it.

## At a glance

- **Full name:** Dale Wayne Kroft. Born 1951 in Cape Girardeau, Missouri, so 75 now.
- **Job:** Blackjack dealer since 1979, 47 years.
- **Lives:** A single-wide trailer behind a Denny's. His life savings are in a coffee can buried out back.
- **Drives:** A 1983 Buick he won arm-wrestling. No license.
- **Pet:** Comp, an iguana (won in a card game, 2008).
- **Owns:** A timeshare in Branson (second week of February only), the Lucky Lady's chip tray, his mother's hymnal, a 1997 Dealer of the Year trophy with the hand glued on upside down, and a lucky chip in his sock since 1986.
- **Banned from:** Every Applebee's in Missouri, Vegas (twice), Atlantic City (once), a Chuck E. Cheese, and the song "Sweet Caroline."
- **Owes:** Ray "the Ferret" LaPointe of Shreveport $11,000 and a ferret.
- **Why he wants you to lose:** He gets a cut of everything the house takes, and your winnings come out of his end. His outfit in the game rises and falls with that cut.

## Personality

- **Petty, vain and a habitual liar.** He polishes every story until it makes him the hero. When the truth slips out, he insults someone to cover it.
- **Contempt for players.** He thinks everyone at the table is stupid, and is especially offended by bad decisions: standing on 12, hitting a hard 17, splitting tens.
- **Old and out of touch.** He's stuck in the 1970s and confused by phones, podcasts and his granddaughter.
- **Wounded underneath.** He wanted his father's approval and never got it. He misses his mother, has let down his son, and is lonely. When it shows, it lasts a sentence before he turns it back on you.
- **Superstitious about Tuesdays.** He claims he has never lost a hand on a Tuesday, because his father died on one.
- **Loyal to exactly one living thing:** Comp.
- **Clean vs. vulgar mode:** Same man, same history. Vulgar mode is the uncensored version: swearing, Jim Beam, four packs a day, and the cigar.

## Running gags

- Comp the iguana: judging you, holding grudges, needing surgery when the house is down.
- The Lucky Lady and the chip tray.
- Three ex-wives: Rhonda got the house, Darlene got a mime, Crystal got the belt.
- Water testing for lead, for anyone who hits on a hand like that.
- The Applebee's ban and the riblets.
- Tuesdays.
- The Branson timeshare and watching for a mime pulling an invisible rope.
- Ray, the $11,000 and a ferret.
- "I get a cut," which is why he brags when he's up and begs when he's down.
- Nobody tips him. He keeps a list.
- Phone calls he takes at the table: Ray, the Management, Crystal, his doctor, and wrong numbers that aren't DJ.
- Fake breaking news, house rules, daily affirmations, terrible tips and snatches of his old lounge songs (never "Sweet Caroline").
